Inputs

Exact critical effect is a forward design quantity. It is not a confidence bound, observed evidence, clinical MCID, or study-specific sample-size calculation.

Check the inputs

    Effect and precision

    The CI reconstructs current SE under a nominal 95% Wald approximation.

    Ratio-measure SE must be entered on the log scale.

    In CI mode, a supplied estimate must agree with the CI midpoint within the released Core tolerance. It is never used as “observed power.”

    Detectability rule

    The app evaluates this scenario but does not validate its scientific or clinical importance.

    Scenario SE = current SE / √multiplier. This is not automatically a sample-size multiplier.

    Optional display range

    Leave both blank for a range that includes the scientific markers. Values are on the natural display scale.

      Calculate to populate the exact critical values, selected-claim probability curve, reference table, and exports.

      Scope

      Educational and research-facing one-parameter Wald design aid. Not a validated clinical or regulatory device and not a study-specific sample-size calculator.

      Privacy

      Inputs remain in page and worker memory. There is no backend, telemetry, persistence, cookie, or input-bearing URL.

      Perugini et al., AMPS 2025 for design rationale; transparent formulas and released Core behavior govern the calculation.
